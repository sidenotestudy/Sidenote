## Part 1: Multiple Choice (40 questions)

**Male Reproductive System & Spermatogenesis**

1. Where does spermatogenesis (sperm production) primarily occur?
   A. Epididymis
   B. Seminiferous tubules of the testes
   C. Prostate gland
   D. Vas deferens

2. The hormone primarily responsible for stimulating sperm production in the testes is:
   A. Estrogen
   B. Testosterone
   C. Progesterone
   D. Oxytocin

3. Sperm cells mature and are stored in the:
   A. Epididymis
   B. Prostate gland
   C. Seminal vesicle
   D. Urethra

4. Which gland contributes fluid that nourishes sperm and forms a large part of semen volume?
   A. Testes
   B. Seminal vesicles
   C. Epididymis
   D. Scrotum

5. The scrotum's primary function is to:
   A. Produce testosterone directly
   B. Keep the testes at a slightly lower temperature than body temperature for optimal sperm production
   C. Store mature sperm indefinitely
   D. Produce semen

6. Meiosis in the male reproductive system produces sperm cells that are:
   A. Diploid, identical to body cells
   B. Haploid, containing half the normal chromosome number
   C. Triploid
   D. Non-functional

   A. Sperm and testosterone

   A. Cervix
   B. Endometrium
   C. Myometrium
   D. Fallopian tube

    A. It continues to thicken indefinitely
    B. It is shed, resulting in menstruation
    C. It immediately becomes the placenta
    D. It converts into the corpus luteum

11. Which structure connects the uterus to the vagina?
    A. Fallopian tube

    B. It typically produces one functional egg and non-functional polar bodies per parent cell
    C. It occurs continuously throughout life at the same rate as spermatogenesis
    D. It does not involve meiosis
